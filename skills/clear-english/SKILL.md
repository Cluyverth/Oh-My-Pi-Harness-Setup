description: Make written English clear, natural, grammatically correct, professional, and faithful to its source. Use whenever an agent or subagent must write or edit English documentation.
---

# Clear English

- Preserve the source meaning and factual detail.
- Use natural, grammatically correct English.
- Prefer direct sentences and precise words.
- Keep a professional, neutral tone.
- Remove filler, repetition, vague phrasing, and unnecessary commentary.
- Do not use dashes where a sentence, comma, colon, or parentheses reads more naturally.
- Do not invent decisions, explanations, motives, or conclusions.
- Quote exact questions faithfully when a documentation template requires exact wording. Do not rewrite quoted material merely to improve its English.
